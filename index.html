Ring Sizer

Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Ring Sizer is an Android app that helps you measure your ring size on your phone screen. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

  • Your measurements, screen setup and saved ring size stay on your phone. We never see them.
  • Ring Sizer has no account, and asks for no camera, location, contacts or storage permissions.
  • The app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information that stays on your device

Ring Sizer keeps a few settings in its private storage: your screen setup, your preferred sizing system and, if you choose to save it, your ring size. These are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Screen setup uses a ruler or a plastic card held against the glass. Nothing is photographed, scanned or recorded.

If Android backup is turned on, these settings may be included in your personal Google backup, like those of any other app. That backup is controlled by your Google account, not by us.

Advertising

Ring Sizer uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner ads. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information such as crash logs and app performance

This data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Ring Sizer asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Ads are not shown on the measuring screens, and we ask Google to show only ads suitable for a general audience.

Sharing

We don't sell your information. If you tap Share, the app hands a short text with your size to the app you choose. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
